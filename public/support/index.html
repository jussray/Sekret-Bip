Support

You can contact Se'kret Bip support without signing in.

Email: support@sekretbip.net

Safety or Circle concerns

Use the in-app report or block controls when available. You can also email support with a description of the issue. Do not include passwords, private journal text, or other sensitive account information in email.

Privacy and account help

For privacy, account access, or deletion questions, use the same support email or review the privacy and safety overview.
