How Se'kret Bip works

Se'kret Bip starts by separating the Teen experience from Bip Jr + Family, then uses account setup and age-appropriate permissions to decide what each person can access.

Short answer: choose the doorway that fits the person using Se'kret Bip, complete the corresponding setup, then continue into the private experience for that account path. Teen entry begins with age setup. Bip Jr + Family begins with family setup alongside a grown-up.

The four-step path

Choose Teen or Bip Jr + Family

The public welcome screen keeps the two entry experiences distinct. People can switch between them before continuing.

Complete age-aware setup

Teen entry continues to an age question before account fields. Bip Jr + Family continues to family setup with a grown-up.

Create an account or sign in

Returning users can sign in through the matching account side. Account setup and age-appropriate permissions determine what each person can access.

Enter the private experience

The Teen welcome is built around having room to reflect, talk, breathe, create, or simply be for a minute. The exact experience available depends on the person's account path and permissions.

Questions people ask

Do Teen and Bip Jr use the same setup?

No. They use separate presentation paths. Teen begins with age setup; Bip Jr + Family continues to family setup with a grown-up.

Does the public website expose private activity?

No. The crawlable information pages explain the product. Private application and member routes remain outside the deliberately public discovery surface.
