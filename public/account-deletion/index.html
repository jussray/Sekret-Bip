Delete your account

This page lets you request deletion of your Se'kret Bip account and associated account data even if you no longer have the app installed.

Request deletion

Email support@sekretbip.net with the subject Se'kret Bip account deletion request.

Use the email address connected to the account when possible. Support may need to verify account ownership before processing the request. Do not email your password, private journal entries, or other sensitive content.

What the request covers

The request covers the app account and associated account-owned data subject to the product's verified deletion process. Data that must be retained for a legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, or regulatory reason will be handled according to the applicable retention notice and privacy policy.

In the app

If you can still sign in, you can also start account deletion from account settings. The in-app flow shows the current deletion state and any available cancellation window.
