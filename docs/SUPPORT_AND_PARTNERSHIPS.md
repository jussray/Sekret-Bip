# Support and partnerships

Se'kret Bip is being built as a privacy-first emotional growth and self-expression product for teens, with an optional, relationship-based parent experience.

## Current support priorities

Financial and strategic support should advance work that directly improves product safety, accessibility, and readiness:

- independent privacy and security review
- teen and parent usability research
- accessibility testing and remediation
- parent-side completion and end-to-end Bridge verification
- moderation and safeguarding design
- release engineering and device testing
- infrastructure, AI, voice, storage, and notification costs
- pilot preparation with qualified youth-serving organizations

## Strong partnership fit

The strongest partners are aligned with one or more of the following:

- youth wellbeing
- privacy-preserving technology
- healthy family communication
- digital literacy and education
- accessibility
- responsible AI
- founder support for underrepresented builders

Useful forms of support include:

- product-development sponsorship
- cloud and developer-tool credits
- privacy, security, accessibility, or safeguarding expertise
- pilot partnerships
- introductions to aligned grantmakers and early-stage funders

## Funding principles

Support must not:

- purchase access to teen data
- weaken privacy boundaries
- create surveillance-based parent access
- override teen consent and intentional-sharing rules
- introduce advertising models based on private emotional data
- require claims that exceed the product's verified implementation state

## Commitment truth

Paid partner, vendor, contractor, or pilot work must not be treated as active unless the required funding is confirmed and founder-authorized.

If funding becomes unavailable:

- pause future paid work and new spending rather than allowing an unfunded commitment to keep expanding;
- communicate the change promptly and do not invent or promise a payment date that is not supportable;
- distinguish work already completed or accepted from future or unperformed work;
- record any completed-work obligation and restart condition in a private operations record, not in the public product repository;
- resume paid work only after the founder explicitly reauthorizes both the budget and the scope.

This repository may record the durable control, but it must not contain private contractor financial details, personal financial circumstances, or unnecessary identifying information.

## Current stage

The product is under active development. The teen experience, parent experience, backend, safety boundaries, and release systems are not all production-complete.

The repository's readiness gates and current-status documents are the source of truth. Support materials and demonstrations must not present blocked or roadmap features as complete.
