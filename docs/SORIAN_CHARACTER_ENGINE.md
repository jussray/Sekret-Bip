## Governing rule

The characters' abilities are not generic superhero powers.

They are **Sorian perception made visible**.

A gift may reveal enough for a character to make a choice. It must not solve the moral, relational, or practical choice for them.

The shared six-character developmental flow is:

```text
Notice → Feel → Understand → Connect → Imagine → Act
```

The durable story engine is:

```text
Gift → distortion → consequence → recognition → choice → growth
```

Soria responds to **who a person is becoming**, not merely whether they are happy or sad.

```text
inner patterns alter outer possibilities
```

Storm is not bad. Sun is not good. Darkness is not failure. Brightness is not virtue. Weather, architecture, pathways, light, sound, and magical response must express the deeper pattern, relationship, discovery, boundary, or choice in play.

## Core six architecture

| Pair | Shared function | Shared symbol | Core question |
|---|---|---|---|
| Night + Nyra | **Awareness** | **Crescent Eye** | What are we actually noticing? |
| Suhana + Suhan | **Belonging** | **Open Circle** | How do we make room without losing ourselves? |
| Sy + Sya | **Discovery** | **Split Star** | What exists, and what could exist? |

The pair symbols are subtle recognition fingerprints, not giant superhero logos. They may appear as a pendant, clasp, zipper pull, embroidery, bracelet, shoe detail, stitching, light behavior, or another restrained continuity marker.

## Night + Nyra — Awareness

### Night — The Watcher

**Gift:** notices external changes, danger, inconsistencies, broken patterns, and what no longer fits.

**Magic direction:** outside → threat / pattern.

**Shadow:** suspicion.

Night's growth pressure is learning that seeing a possibility does not make it the truth. Awareness must not harden into certainty before evidence arrives.

Soria may answer healthy awareness by clarifying hidden structure, reflections, paths, or anomalies. When suspicion takes over, too many signals may appear at once so the real task becomes discernment rather than detection.

### Nyra — The Intuitive

**Gift:** notices emotional undercurrents and what changed between people.

**Magic direction:** inside → emotion.

**Shadow:** over-reading.

Nyra's growth pressure is learning that feelings are information, not verdicts. Sensing tension does not grant access to another person's private thoughts or motives.

Soria may express emotional currents through restrained light, sound, reflection, or atmosphere. When Nyra becomes too certain, several plausible meanings may appear at once, forcing curiosity and consent instead of mind-reading.

### Pair truth

```text
Night notices what changed around us.
Nyra notices what changed between us.
```

Together, they must learn not to confuse awareness with omniscience.

## Suhana + Suhan — Belonging

### Suhana — The Center

**Gift:** makes room for feelings to exist, become nameable, and move toward expression.

**Magic direction:** feeling → expression.

**Shadow:** over-carrying.

Suhana's growth pressure is learning that empathy does not make her responsible for managing everybody else's emotional state.

Soria may answer healthy emotional space by making shared environments easier to navigate. When Suhana carries too much, rooms, voices, lights, or paths may crowd toward one center until the burden becomes visible.

### Suhan — The Steady

**Gift:** makes room for a person to remain present through discomfort, conflict, and imperfection.

**Magic direction:** person → connection.

**Shadow:** staying too long.

Suhan's growth pressure is learning that staying is not always the same thing as loving. Some relationships require repair, distance, changed terms, or an ending.

Soria may answer healthy steadiness by stabilizing a space long enough for honest contact. When steadiness becomes refusal to change, broken structures may remain standing, old paths may stay lit too long, or doors may refuse to close.

### Pair truth

```text
Suhana makes room for the feeling.
Suhan makes room for the person.
```

Belonging requires boundaries as well as warmth.

## Sy + Sya — Discovery

### Sy — The Mind

**Gift:** understands systems, mechanisms, rules, repeating patterns, and why something behaves the way it does.

**Magic direction:** reality → rule.

**Shadow:** over-analysis.

Sy's growth pressure is learning when more information stops creating clarity and starts postponing courage.

Soria may answer healthy analysis by revealing mechanisms, geometry, or routes. When analysis becomes avoidance, maps can contain more maps, rules can reveal more rules, and the useful question becomes which information actually changes the decision.

### Sya — The Imagineer

**Gift:** sees alternate uses, routes, interpretations, openings, and futures.

**Magic direction:** rule → possibility.

**Shadow:** scattering.

Sya's growth pressure is learning that possibility becomes power only when something is chosen.

Soria may answer useful imagination by briefly revealing alternate paths. When possibility becomes escape, doors, stairs, routes, or futures may multiply faster than anyone can follow them.

### Pair truth

```text
Sy discovers the system.
Sya discovers the opening.
```

Together, they decide which opening deserves to become real.

## Cloud / The Calm — Presence

Cloud / The Calm sits **outside** the six-character pairing structure.

Cloud is not step seven in `Notice → Feel → Understand → Connect → Imagine → Act`.

Cloud's function is **Presence**: creating enough room for the other characters to notice, feel, understand, connect, imagine, and act without being captured by the first reaction.

**Gift:** calm presence.

**Shadow:** passivity.

Cloud's long-form growth pressure is learning that neutrality is not automatically wisdom.

```text
Calm is not having no weather.
Calm is knowing the weather does not have to choose for you.
```

And:

```text
Calm is not refusing to enter the storm.
Calm is knowing why you are entering it.
```

Cloud begins by believing wisdom can mean not being moved. Cloud grows by learning that sometimes character means choosing what is worth moving for.

Cloud / The Calm remains one specific sky-kin individual within the larger cloud civilization. Birth-cloud names a relationship, not Cloud's species or job title. Cloud learned from many pairings rather than being assigned to one child. Cloud may draw from birth-cloud culture without claiming to be the user's birth-cloud or a replacement for real relationships.

## Relationship engine

A character does not become "less themselves" to grow. Their gift becomes wiser.

| Character | Gift | Distortion / shadow | Growth pressure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Night | External pattern awareness | Suspicion | Evidence before certainty |
| Nyra | Emotional undercurrent awareness | Over-reading | Ask instead of assume |
| Suhana | Makes room for feeling | Over-carrying | Care without self-erasure |
| Suhan | Makes room for people | Staying too long | Loyalty with boundaries |
| Sy | Understands systems | Over-analysis | Know when understanding is enough to act |
| Sya | Sees possibilities | Scattering | Choose which possibility becomes real |
| Cloud | Creates calm / presence | Passivity | Know when presence requires action |

Episode conflict should come from gifts and shadows interacting, not from arbitrary personality fights.

Examples:

- Night notices danger before Sy can prove it.
- Nyra senses hurt but may misread its cause.
- Suhana understands why someone is hurting while Night still names harmful behavior.
- Suhan keeps a relationship open after others would leave, but may need to recognize when endurance is enabling harm.
- Sy wants coherence while Sya keeps opening possibilities.
- Cloud can lower pressure, but may need to stop waiting when action is required.

## Soria response law

Soria does not award points for being "good."

It reveals consequences and possibilities.

A path may open. Another may disappear. A crowded room may empty. A beautiful structure may crack. A frightening landscape may finally make sense. Something comforting may become restrictive. Something frightening may reveal freedom.

The recurring story question is:

```text
Who are you becoming while dealing with this?
```

Soria's answer is environmental, relational, and causal rather than moralizing.

## Runtime boundary

The current product companion runtime remains governed by its own named-companion contracts. This story engine does **not** automatically add Nyra, Suhan, or Sya to:

- `CompanionId` unions;
- onboarding choices;
- persisted profile/state keys;
- AI provider prompts;
- routes;
- analytics;
- parent surfaces;
- voice assets;
- character pickers.

Adding a story character to product runtime requires a separate founder-approved product, privacy, identity, asset, route, persistence, and proof decision.

Cloud is already a named product companion, but the runtime must describe Cloud consistently with cloud-civilization canon: **Cloud / The Calm is one sky-kin individual; birth-clouds are relationships.**

## Production fail conditions

A story, image, prompt, or animation fails this character engine if it:

- turns the gifts into generic combat/superhero powers;
- makes one ability solve the choice for everyone;
- treats intuition as mind-reading;
- treats empathy as responsibility for everyone else's emotions;
- treats loyalty as an obligation to remain in any relationship;
- treats analysis as automatically wiser than action;
- treats imagination as automatically better than commitment;
- treats calm as silence in the face of harm;
- treats storm/darkness as moral failure or brightness/sun as moral goodness;
- enrolls Nyra, Suhan, or Sya into product runtime merely because they exist in story canon;
- describes Cloud / The Calm as the only cloud person, as everybody's birth-cloud, or as a pet/owned familiar.
