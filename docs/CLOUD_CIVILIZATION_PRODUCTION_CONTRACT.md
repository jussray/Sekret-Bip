# Cloud Civilization Production Contract

Status: **CANONICAL PRODUCTION EXTENSION**

Read with:
- `docs/CLOUD_ORIGIN_CANON.md`
- `docs/SORIA_CANON.md`
- `sekret-bip-world-bible.md`
- the active episode file under `season-*`

This file does not replace Cloud's origin canon. It makes the already-established sky-family and birth-cloud world rules explicit for visual production so a generator cannot flatten cloud people into generic mascots.

## Core law

```text
Cloud people are a civilization.
Birth-clouds are relationships.
Cloud / The Calm is one specific sky-kin individual.
```

A cloud person is not an accessory attached to a Sorian person.

Sky-kin have their own:
- identities and personalities;
- parents, siblings, extended families, and chosen family;
- lineages and customs;
- neighborhoods, communities, cloud cities, and sky homes;
- friendships, pair-bonds, obligations, memories, and lives outside a birth partnership;
- forms, color languages, weather signatures, and abilities.

Narrative authority for Cloud's already-established family and home remains `docs/CLOUD_ORIGIN_CANON.md`, including the Lowlight Drift and Cloud's existing sky-family. Do not invent a new named cloud relative, sibling, city, or lineage merely to fill a shot.

## Birth-partner bond

Every Sorian begins life with a birth-cloud bond in canon.

The bond connects two people. It is not ownership, pet logic, servitude, or a replacement for family, friendship, faith, judgment, or self-trust.

A birth-cloud keeps its own family, history, community, preferences, and agency while becoming deeply linked to the Sorian person's life.

A birth-cloud may be nearby, distant, quiet, weather-like, or visually absent in a particular scene without the underlying bond ceasing to exist.

## Weather signature

Every cloud person can have a distinct weather signature.

Within that individual's nature and abilities, the cloud may shift:
- color and glow;
- shape, density, scale, or texture;
- mist, wind, rain, snow, thunder, temperature, or other weather-like expression;
- the local atmosphere around itself and its bonded Sorian.

Weather can resonate with broad emotional state, relationship state, danger, discovery, concentration, power use, or relief.

Weather is **not mind-reading** and must not publicly reveal a person's private thoughts. The visual rule is emotional infrastructure, not surveillance.

## Power circuit

Some Sorian magic may route through the birth-cloud relationship.

```text
SORIAN PERSON ↔ BIRTH-CLOUD
                ↓
       shared magical circuit
```

Depending on the individual pairing, a birth-cloud may act as a **source, conduit, amplifier, stabilizer, or counterweight** for a Sorian ability.

The cloud is never a disposable battery or weapon owned by the person.

The person still chooses and acts. The cloud keeps judgment and agency. A cloud may warn, question, steady, refuse, redirect, or become the quiet voice of reason when its birth partner is moving too fast or using power badly.

## Pair-bond weather law

When two Sorians form a chosen life-pair or family bond, their existing birth-clouds form a linked weather bond too.

```text
Person A ═ Person B
   │          │
Cloud A ═══ Cloud B
```

The cloud-to-cloud link does not erase either cloud's original family, siblings, lineage, home, personality, or independent relationships.

The linked clouds may coordinate weather, share magical resonance, disagree, protect boundaries, or develop their own relationship over time.

A child born into that family receives a distinct birth-cloud bond. The child's cloud is its own cloud person with its own sky-family lineage. It is not automatically a copy, mixture, or inheritance of either parent's cloud.

## Cloud / The Calm

`@CLOUD_CANON` identifies **Cloud / The Calm**, one specific cloud person in the Se'kret Bip cast.

Cloud is unusual in established canon because Cloud learned from many pairings rather than being assigned to one child.

Therefore production must never imply:
- Cloud is the only cloud person in Soria;
- Cloud is simultaneously Night's, Suhana's, and Sy's personal birth-cloud;
- all cloud people share Cloud's face, color, personality, role, or abilities;
- a generic cloud can substitute for an unbound canonical cloud relative or birth partner.

## Visual-production gate

Before a named or prominently identifiable cloud person appears:

1. identify the cloud by canon role/name;
2. bind an approved character-authority reference/fingerprint;
3. verify family/lineage continuity when relevant;
4. verify its individual weather signature;
5. verify exact cast count;
6. reject generic cloud substitutions.

If a Sorian character's exact birth-cloud identity has not been visually bound yet, preserve the bond through **non-identifying weather/color/mood-light cues** only. Do not invent a face, sibling, name, costume, or cloud body to fill the frame.

## Hard fail conditions

A frame fails cloud canon if it:
- treats a cloud as a pet, prop, battery, or owned familiar;
- implies Cloud / The Calm represents every cloud person;
- invents an unapproved named cloud relative, sibling, city, or birth partner;
- uses weather to expose private thought as public fact;
- merges two bonded clouds into one identity;
- gives a child a clone of a parent's cloud by default;
- spends video credits before an identifiable cloud character passes the same still-image fingerprint gate as every other canonical character.
