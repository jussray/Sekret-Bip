## § 1 — Information We Collect

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|

|---|---|
| Device data | Device model, OS version, app version |

## § 2 — How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to:

1. Create and manage your account
2. Deliver core app features (mood tracking, journaling, AI companion, rooms)
3. Personalize your experience (themes, content suggestions)
4. Send service notifications and safety alerts
5. Respond to support requests
6. Analyze usage patterns to improve the Service
7. Comply with legal obligations
8. Protect user safety and prevent abuse

**We do not sell your personal information.** We do not use personal information for behavioral advertising.

## § 3 — Sharing & Disclosure

We may share personal information with:

| Recipient | Purpose | Safeguards |
|---|---|---|
| **Infrastructure providers** (Supabase, Cloudflare, Vercel) | Hosting, database, CDN | Data processing agreements; encryption |
| **AI provider** (OpenAI) | AI companion feature | Anonymized prompts; zero-retention API tier |
| **Push notification provider** (Expo) | Delivering notifications | Push token only; no message content |
| **Analytics** (if any, TBD) | Usage analysis | Aggregate/anonymized data only |
| **Law enforcement / legal process** | Valid legal orders | Minimum necessary; we notify users when legally permitted |
| **Safety organizations** | Imminent threat to life | Crisis-related data only |

We do not share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

## § 4 — Data Retention

- **Account data:** Retained while your account is active plus 30 days post-deletion request
- **Content (journals, mood logs):** Retained while active; deleted within 30 days of account deletion
- **Log/analytics data:** 90 days rolling retention
- **Legal holds:** Data subject to a legal hold is retained until the hold is lifted

## § 5 — Your Rights

### For All Users

- **Access:** Request a copy of your personal data
- **Correction:** Update inaccurate information in-app or by contacting us
- **Deletion:** Delete your account in-app or email us; we process within 30 days
- **Portability:** Request your data in a machine-readable format
- **Objection:** Object to certain processing activities

### California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)

California residents have additional rights:

- Right to know the categories and specific pieces of personal information collected
- Right to delete
- Right to opt out of sale (we do not sell — this right is automatically satisfied)
- Right to non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights
- Right to correct inaccurate personal information
- Right to limit use of sensitive personal information

To submit a CCPA request, email **[TBD — privacy@bip.app]** with subject line "CCPA Request."

### Minors (Under 18 — California)

California minors may request removal of content they posted publicly. Contact **[TBD — privacy@bip.app]** with the specific content and your username.

## § 6 — Security

- AES-256 encryption at rest

- Regular security audits

No system is perfectly secure. If you discover a vulnerability, please report it to **[TBD — security@bip.app]**.
