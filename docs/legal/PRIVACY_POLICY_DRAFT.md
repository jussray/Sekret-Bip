# Se'kret Bip Privacy Policy — Draft

**Draft version — June 2026**

> This draft is for product and legal review. It must not be published as final legal text without confirming that the deployed app, database, storage, analytics, vendors, and deletion behavior match it.

## 1. Who We Serve

Se'kret Bip is intended for users ages 13–17 and for parents or guardians using the separate Parent Window. Users under 13 may not create an account.

## 2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features used, Se'kret Bip may process:

- account information such as email, nickname, age eligibility, and account preferences;
- profile and Circle identity information such as Bip ID, avatar, pronouns, display name, and support preferences;
- private Pages, attachments, saved companion replies, and voice reflections;
- mood check-ins and calm activity;
- Circle posts, reactions, Crew relationships, and check-ins;
- Bippin 2 points, streaks, rewards, milestones, and growth activity;
- teen-selected Bridge shares and parent responses;
- parent-authored Pages, Parent Circle posts, voice reflections, and support activity;
- technical and security information needed to operate and protect the service.

Se'kret Bip does not intend to collect precise location, address-book contacts, or advertising identifiers for behavioral advertising.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

- provide account, journaling, voice, community, growth, and support features;
- personalize companions, themes, prompts, and room experiences;
- generate AI companion responses;
- maintain safe memory and milestone markers;
- sync data across the user's authorized devices;
- enforce privacy, consent, and parent/teen boundaries;
- detect abuse, fraud, security incidents, or safety risks;
- comply with legal obligations.

## 4. Teen Privacy and the Parent Window

Private teen content is not automatically available to a linked parent.

Parents may receive only:

- items a teen intentionally shares;
- summaries or aggregates authorized for parent visibility;
- parent-authored records;
- support prompts and responses allowed by the Bridge design.

Parents may not browse raw private journals, raw private voice reflections, private companion memory, period information, or unshared teen content.

## 5. Circle and Crew Identity

A user's Circle identity may be separate from their private account identity. Circle and Crew may display an anonymous or chosen display name, avatar, support preferences, mood/weather status, and other fields the user chooses to make visible in that context.

Acceptance into a Bip Crew does not grant access to private Pages, voice recordings, parent information, or private account data.

## 6. Memories

Profile Memories are safe timeline or scrapbook markers created from activity across Bip. The feature may show that a milestone or activity occurred, such as joining Crew, reaching a growth milestone, or writing Pages.

Profile Memories do not copy raw private journal text, raw voice content, or unshared private reflections by default. Parent Memories reflect the parent's own support activity and do not reveal a teen's private activity.

## 7. AI Services

Se'kret Bip may send relevant prompt content to an API-based AI provider to generate companion responses.

AI interactions are transient by default. A response may be stored when the user intentionally saves it as part of a Page, reflection, voice artifact, memory marker, or other account-owned feature.

AI services are not emergency or clinical services. Users should contact local emergency services or regionally appropriate crisis support when immediate safety is at risk.

## 8. Service Providers

Se'kret Bip may use service providers for authentication, database storage, file storage, API routing, security, and AI processing. Planned or current providers may include Supabase, Cloudflare, and OpenAI.

These providers process information on Se'kret Bip's behalf according to their contracts and product settings. Se'kret Bip does not authorize service providers to use teen content for behavioral advertising.

## 9. Data Sharing

Se'kret Bip does not sell personal information.

Information may be disclosed:

- when the user intentionally shares it through a product feature;
- to processors necessary to operate the service;
- to protect users, investigate abuse, or respond to a valid legal request;
- as part of a business transaction, subject to applicable privacy protections and notice requirements.

## 10. Data Retention

Information should be retained only as long as needed to provide the feature, protect the service, meet user expectations, or satisfy legal obligations.

Production retention periods must be documented before launch for account data, Pages, voice files, Circle/Crew content, Bridge shares, AI processing logs, security logs, and deleted-account backups.

## 11. Account Access, Correction, and Deletion

Users should be able to review and correct core account information and request deletion from within the app.

Deletion must cover associated database records and private storage objects, including journals, voice artifacts, Circle identity, Crew records, Bridge records, memories, and growth history, except where limited retention is legally required.

The production deletion flow is a launch requirement and must be verified before this draft is published as final.

## 12. Security

Se'kret Bip uses technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect user information, including encrypted transport, access controls, private storage, server-side secrets, and parent/teen separation policies.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Se'kret Bip must maintain an incident-response process before launch.

## 13. Children Under 13

Se'kret Bip is not intended for children under 13. If Se'kret Bip learns that it has collected personal information from a child under 13 without an authorized legal basis and required consent, it will take steps to delete the information and close the account.

## 14. Changes to This Policy

Material changes should be communicated in age-appropriate language. Where required, renewed consent or acknowledgment will be requested.

## 15. Contact

A monitored privacy contact address and business mailing address must be inserted before publication.
