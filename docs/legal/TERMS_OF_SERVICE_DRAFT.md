# Se'kret Bip Terms of Service — Draft

**Draft version — June 2026**

> This draft is for product and legal review. It must not be published as final legal text until business identity, contact details, governing law, subscription terms, and deployed product behavior are confirmed.

## 1. Who May Use Se'kret Bip

Se'kret Bip is intended for users ages 13–17 and adults using the Parent Window. You must be at least 13 years old to create an account. Do not use the service if you are under 13.

By creating an account, you confirm that the information you provide about age eligibility is accurate.

## 2. What Se'kret Bip Provides

Se'kret Bip provides private reflection, voice, community, growth, companion, and optional parent-support tools. Features may include Pages, Voice Bip, Calm, Circle, Crew, Bippin 2, Bridge, Profile, Memories, and Parent Window equivalents.

Features may change as the product develops.

## 3. Se'kret Bip Is Not Emergency or Medical Care

Se'kret Bip and its AI companions are not therapists, doctors, emergency responders, or substitutes for professional care.

When you or someone else may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or an appropriate crisis resource in your region and seek help from a trusted person nearby.

## 4. Teen Privacy and Parent Support

Teen-private journals, private voice reflections, private companion content, period information, and unshared reflections are not automatically available to a linked parent.

Parents may receive information only through teen-selected sharing, allowed summaries or aggregates, and parent-authored support features according to the product's privacy settings.

A parent link does not give a parent ownership of the teen's private account content.

## 5. Circle and Crew Conduct

Users must not use Circle, Crew, or other community tools to:

- bully, threaten, harass, shame, or exploit another person;
- solicit sexual content or sexual interaction involving minors;
- share another person's private information without permission;
- impersonate another person;
- encourage self-harm, violence, illegal activity, or dangerous challenges;
- evade blocks, account restrictions, or safety controls;
- use Bip IDs, invitations, or messaging for spam or commercial solicitation.

Se'kret Bip may restrict or remove content or accounts to protect users and the service.

## 6. Your Content

You keep ownership of content you create. You give Se'kret Bip a limited permission to host, process, transmit, and display that content only as needed to operate the features you choose.

Sharing content through Circle, Crew, or Bridge makes it visible only within the selected context and permissions. You are responsible for choosing what to share.

## 7. AI-Generated Content

Companion responses are generated using AI and may be incomplete, inaccurate, or inappropriate despite safety controls. Do not rely on AI responses for medical, legal, financial, or emergency decisions.

AI interactions are transient by default, but responses may be saved when you intentionally add them to a Page, reflection, voice artifact, memory marker, or other account-owned feature.

## 8. Accounts and Security

Keep your login information private. Notify Se'kret Bip if you believe your account has been accessed without permission.

Do not attempt to access another user's private data, bypass age restrictions, defeat privacy controls, probe private APIs, or misuse service credentials.

## 9. Subscriptions and Rewards

Any paid subscription, merchandise, or reward-redemption terms must be shown before purchase or redemption. Points and rewards have no cash value unless the product explicitly states otherwise.

Parent approval requirements for purchases, rewards, or fulfillment must be clearly disclosed in the relevant flow.

## 10. Account Suspension and Termination

Se'kret Bip may suspend or close accounts that violate these terms, create safety risks, misuse community features, or threaten the security of the service.

When practical and safe, users should receive a clear explanation and a way to appeal.

## 11. Deleting Your Account

Users may request deletion through the in-app deletion flow once it is available for production launch. Deletion must cover associated database records and private storage according to the Privacy Policy and Data Deletion Requirements.

Some narrowly limited information may be retained when legally required or necessary for security, fraud prevention, billing, or child-safety obligations.

## 12. Changes

Material changes should be explained in age-appropriate language. Continued use after an effective date may constitute acceptance where permitted, but renewed consent or acknowledgment will be requested when required.
