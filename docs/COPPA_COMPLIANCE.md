## § 2 — Age Gate & Parental Consent

### 2.1 Age Verification at Registration

Bip collects date of birth during account registration. Users identified as under 13 years of age ("Children") are placed into a **restricted access flow** that requires **verifiable parental consent (VPC)** before any account is created or any personal information is collected beyond what is strictly necessary to obtain that consent.

### 2.2 Verifiable Parental Consent Methods

We use one or more of the following FTC-approved VPC methods:

- **Email Plus:** A consent email is sent to the parent email provided; a follow-up confirmation (e.g., toll-free number call, credit card micro-transaction, or signed consent form return) is required within 72 hours.
- **Credit/Debit Card Verification:** A nominal $0.00–$0.50 authorization (immediately reversed) confirms the parent's identity.
- **Government ID Upload (optional, for high-data features):** Parent uploads a photo ID through an encrypted, third-party identity verification service. ID is not retained after verification.

### 2.3 What Happens Before Consent Is Obtained

Prior to VPC, we collect **only**:

- Parent/guardian name
- Parent/guardian email address
- Child's first name or username (for internal reference only)

No personal information about the Child is shared with third parties, used for behavioral advertising, or retained beyond 30 days if consent is not completed.

## § 3 — Information We Collect from Children

Once verifiable parental consent is obtained, we may collect the following information from Children:

| Category | Examples | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Account Information | Username, avatar, nickname | App functionality |
| Mood & Emotional Data | Mood logs, journal entries, check-ins | Core wellness features |
| Usage Data | Feature interactions, session length | App improvement |
| Device Information | Device type, OS version | Technical support |
| Communications | Messages to AI companion (processed, not stored in identifiable form) | Feature delivery |

### 3.1 What We Do NOT Collect from Children

- Full legal name (not required)
- Precise geolocation (GPS coordinates)
- Photos or videos containing biometric identifiers
- Social Security numbers or government IDs
- Financial information
- Persistent identifiers used for behavioral advertising across third-party sites

---

## § 4 — How We Use Children's Information

We use Children's personal information solely to:

1. Provide, maintain, and improve Bip's core features
2. Respond to support requests
3. Send service notifications (to parent email only for under-13 users)
4. Comply with legal obligations
5. Protect the safety and security of users

We **do not** use Children's information for:

- Targeted or behavioral advertising
- Sale or rental to third parties
- Building profiles for commercial purposes beyond app functionality
- Training external AI models without separate, explicit written parental consent

---

## § 5 — Disclosure to Third Parties

We do not disclose Children's personal information to third parties except:

| Recipient Type | Purpose | Data Shared |
|---|---|---|
| Cloud Infrastructure (e.g., Supabase, Cloudflare) | Data storage and delivery | Encrypted data; no raw PII |
| AI Processing (e.g., OpenAI) | AI companion responses | Anonymized/pseudonymized prompts only; zero-data-retention API tier |
| Legal/Regulatory | Compliance with court orders, law enforcement requests | Minimum necessary |
| Safety Partners | Emergency response (e.g., crisis hotline integrations) | Only with immediate safety threat |

All third-party service providers are bound by data processing agreements that prohibit them from using Children's data for their own commercial purposes.

## § 6 — Parental Rights

Parents and legal guardians have the right to:

1. **Review** personal information collected from their Child — contact [TBD — privacy@bip.app]
2. **Correct** inaccurate information
3. **Delete** their Child's account and all associated personal information
4. **Refuse further collection** — we will delete the account and cease data collection within 30 days of request
5. **Opt out of specific disclosures** to third parties (where technically feasible)

To exercise these rights, contact us at **[TBD — privacy@bip.app]** with:
- Parent/guardian name
- Child's username or account email
- Description of the request

We will respond within **15 business days**.

## § 7 — Data Retention

- **Active accounts:** Retained while account is active plus 30 days after deletion request
- **Mood/journal data:** Retained for the period the user account is active; purged within 30 days of account deletion
- **Consent records:** Retained for 5 years from the date of consent (legal compliance requirement)
- **Support communications:** Retained for 2 years

---

## § 8 — Security

We implement reasonable security measures appropriate to the sensitivity of Children's personal information:

- AES-256 encryption at rest
- TLS 1.3 encryption in transit
- Access controls limiting employee access to Children's data
- Annual security audits
- Incident response plan with 72-hour notification to parents of any breach affecting their Child's data

---

## § 9 — Changes to This Policy

Material changes to this COPPA Compliance Policy require renewed verifiable parental consent before those changes take effect for existing Child accounts. Non-material changes will be communicated via email to the parent/guardian email on file with 30 days' notice.
