# Se’kret Bip World Bible

Status: **CANONICAL PRODUCTION AUTHORITY**
Scope: world identity, magic rules, environmental continuity, and world-level visual references.
Does not own: companion identity.

## Canon architecture

```text
Character canon = Who exists
World canon     = Where magic happens
Episode canon   = What this story means
```

The governing principle is:

> **Magic is emotional infrastructure.**

A visual effect is not decoration. Every major magical event must express the emotional state, discovery, choice, or relationship change happening in the story.

## Authority separation

Every visual reference used in production must be classified before generation:

- `character-authority`: may define a canonical character’s face, silhouette, age lane, clothing markers, role, and identity.
- `world-authority`: may define palette, architecture, atmosphere, portal language, magical materials, lighting, landscape, and environmental behavior.
- `episode-authority`: may define the meaning, world rule, action, timing, and continuity required for one episode.
- `exploration`: may inspire only what the founder explicitly approves; it never promotes itself to canon.

**World-authority images must never be used to infer or replace character identity.**
Ensemble posters, portal posters, generated storyboards, and concept art can contain accidental people, faces, clothes, labels, or cast counts. Those incidental people are not companions.

## World-first visual law

Se’kret Bip opens from quiet intimacy into wonder.

Durable visual cues:
- moonlit violet and indigo foundation;
- controlled pink, blue, and soft-gold glow;
- crescent symbols as threshold language;
- stars, mist, lantern warmth, luminous paths, floating or elevated architecture;
- premium painterly dimensional rendering;
- spacious compositions with a safe, curious, emotionally calm feeling;
- magic that reacts to relationship and emotion instead of arbitrary spectacle.

Avoid:
- generic fantasy spectacle with no emotional cause;
- random people used as visual filler;
- accidental replacement companions;
- noisy theme-park motion;
- UI, logos, or generated typography embedded into world keyframes unless the shot explicitly requires them.

## Diversity and culture

The Se’kret Bip universe may contain many skin tones, cultures, hair traditions, clothing influences, homes, landscapes, and community expressions.

That diversity does **not** authorize companion identity drift.

```text
World expression may vary.
Companion identity stays locked.
```

Background inhabitants may be introduced only when an episode explicitly calls for them. They must not be mistaken for Night, Suhana, Sy, Cloud, a parent, or a Bip Jr. character.

## Cloud civilization and birth-bond law

Read `docs/CLOUD_CIVILIZATION_PRODUCTION_CONTRACT.md` with `docs/CLOUD_ORIGIN_CANON.md` before any shot prominently depicts a cloud person.

```text
Cloud people are a civilization.
Birth-clouds are relationships.
Cloud / The Calm is one specific sky-kin individual.
```

Cloud people have their own identities, families, siblings, lineages, communities, cloud cities, relationships, memories, and lives outside their birth partnerships. Existing named Cloud family/home canon remains authoritative in `docs/CLOUD_ORIGIN_CANON.md`; production must not invent a new named cloud relative, sibling, city, or lineage to fill a frame.

Every Sorian begins life with a birth-cloud bond. The bond is mutual relationship, not pet logic, ownership, servitude, or a replacement for the person's judgment.

Each cloud person may have an individual weather signature. Within that cloud's nature and abilities, color, glow, shape, density, mist, wind, rain, snow, thunder, temperature, or nearby atmosphere may shift around the cloud and its bonded Sorian. Weather expresses broad emotional or magical state without publicly exposing private thought.

Some Sorian magic can route through the birth-cloud relationship. Depending on the pairing, the cloud may act as source, conduit, amplifier, stabilizer, or counterweight. The person still chooses and acts; the cloud retains agency and may warn, question, steady, refuse, redirect, or become a quiet voice of reason. A cloud is never a disposable magical battery.

When two Sorians form a chosen life-pair or family bond, their existing birth-clouds form a linked weather bond too. The cloud-to-cloud bond does not erase either cloud's original family, siblings, lineage, home, personality, or independent relationships.

A child receives a distinct birth-cloud relationship. The child's cloud is its own cloud person and lineage, not automatically a copy, mixture, or inheritance of either parent's cloud.

If an exact birth-cloud or named sky-kin identity has not been approved and fingerprint-bound, production may imply the bond only through non-identifying weather/color/mood-light cues. It must not invent a face, cloud body, sibling, name, or substitute character.

## Portal rule

A portal is a threshold created by emotional readiness, attention, or shared action. It should look responsive, not mechanical-only and not random.

For Episode 001, a crescent symbol moves first. Suhana follows it. The portal opens into the Se’kret Bip world.

## Bridge rule

The Bridge That Listens is relationship-responsive infrastructure.

- One character cannot fully activate it alone.
- Bridge fragments flicker when the group is disconnected.
- Shared movement and coordination make the bridge solid.
- The environment visibly changes because the group changes how it acts together.

If the bridge could appear for any unrelated reason, the shot fails canon.
