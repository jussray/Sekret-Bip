## Logline

A moving crescent leads Night, Suhana, Sy, and Cloud into a hidden world where a broken bridge will only form when they learn to move together.

## Cloud civilization continuity

Cloud / The Calm is one specific sky-kin individual. Cloud is not the only cloud in Soria and is not simultaneously Night's, Suhana's, and Sy's personal birth-cloud.

Night, Suhana, and Sy each exist within Soria's birth-cloud tradition. Their exact individual birth-cloud visual identities are **not approved Episode 001 cast references yet**.

Therefore:
- do not generate generic mini-clouds beside Night, Suhana, or Sy;
- do not reuse Cloud / The Calm as their personal birth-cloud;
- do not invent cloud names, faces, siblings, lineages, or bodies for this episode;
- their birth-cloud bonds may register through restrained **non-identifying weather/color/mood-light cues** around them;
- weather cues may change in color, glow, mist, breeze, warmth, rain-light, or other subtle atmospheric behavior without becoming a separate identifiable cloud character;
- weather may express broad emotional or magical resonance but must not expose private thought as public fact.

During the cooperation beat, those separate weather cues can begin out of rhythm and then harmonize as the characters coordinate. This reinforces:

```text
shared movement → shared environment response
```

without inventing new cloud characters.

## Shot prompts

### World keyframe / Shot 3

World-authority only. No invented people or identifiable cloud characters.

A quiet crescent threshold opens into the Se’kret Bip world: moonlit violet and indigo, controlled pink and soft-gold glow, stars and mist, elevated architecture, luminous paths, emotionally calm wonder. The portal feels responsive to discovery rather than mechanically triggered. Leave clean composition space for the approved cast to be introduced from character-authority references later. No text, logo, substitute people, or generic mascot clouds.

### Shot 1

Macro close-up of the crescent symbol gliding subtly across a textured wall. Low, intimate light. The magic feels attentive, almost listening. No full character face is needed.

### Shot 2

Bind exactly `@SUHANA_CANON @NIGHT_CANON @SY_CANON`. Suhana reaches for the moving crescent while Night and Sy react with restrained curiosity. Preserve exact identity, age lane, clothing continuity, and cast count. No Cloud / The Calm yet unless explicitly revised. Their existing birth-cloud relationships may register only as subtle, non-identifying local weather/color/mood-light shifts. No separate cloud face or body.

### Shot 4

Bind exactly `@CLOUD_CANON`. Cloud / The Calm crosses the newly revealed world while the environment wakes gently behind it. Cloud is a specific sky-kin individual from a larger cloud civilization, not a generic species mascot and not the personal birth-cloud of all three teens. No extra people or invented cloud relatives.

### Shot 5

Bind exactly `@NIGHT_CANON @SUHANA_CANON @SY_CANON @CLOUD_CANON`. The group reaches a gap. Bridge fragments appear but cannot stabilize. The visual cause must read as incomplete coordination. Let Night's, Suhana's, and Sy's non-identifying local weather cues feel subtly out of rhythm with one another. Do not add generic birth-cloud bodies.

### Shot 6

Bind exactly `@NIGHT_CANON @SUHANA_CANON @SY_CANON @CLOUD_CANON`. Their movement aligns. The same unstable fragments lock into a solid luminous bridge in response to coordination. Shared action must visibly cause the magic. Their previously separate weather/color/mood-light cues synchronize with the bridge response without turning into new cloud characters.

### Shot 7

Bind exactly `@NIGHT_CANON @SUHANA_CANON @SY_CANON @CLOUD_CANON`. Wide shot as the four cross together and the city/environment wakes. Preserve exact cast count. The atmosphere can carry a harmonized weather resonance around the group, but no unbound birth-cloud identities may appear. Leave clean space for the closing line to be added in post.
