## Logline

A moving crescent leads Night, Suhana, Sy, and Cloud into a hidden world where a broken bridge will only form when they learn to move together.

## Emotional theme

**Belonging through cooperation.**

## World rule

The bridge cannot be activated by one character alone. It forms piece by piece when the group moves together.

## Shot prompts

### World keyframe / Shot 3

World-authority only. No invented people.

A quiet crescent threshold opens into the Se’kret Bip world: moonlit violet and indigo, controlled pink and soft-gold glow, stars and mist, elevated architecture, luminous paths, emotionally calm wonder. The portal feels responsive to discovery rather than mechanically triggered. Leave clean composition space for the approved cast to be introduced from character-authority references later. No text, logo, or substitute people.

### Shot 1

Macro close-up of the crescent symbol gliding subtly across a textured wall. Low, intimate light. The magic feels attentive, almost listening. No full character face is needed.

Bind exactly `@SUHANA_CANON @NIGHT_CANON @SY_CANON`. Suhana reaches for the moving crescent while Night and Sy react with restrained curiosity. Preserve exact identity, age lane, clothing continuity, and cast count. No Cloud yet unless explicitly revised.
