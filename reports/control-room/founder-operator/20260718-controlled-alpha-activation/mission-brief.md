# Controlled-Alpha Founder Mission Brief

## Mission

Fully activate every already-built, launch-relevant Se’kret Bip feature for a controlled teen and parent alpha while preserving privacy, consent, authorization, deletion, rollback, and production isolation.

Founder Control Room is the operating source of truth. GitHub retains repository and review evidence, but work order, artifact status, risks, proof requirements, and founder gates are maintained here.

## 5W1H

- **Who:** the founder owns scope, credentials, paid capacity, deployment, distribution, deletion, merge, and launch decisions. ChatGPT maintains the ordered plan. Codex owns narrow repository implementation. GitHub, Playwright, Cloudflare, Supabase, and the local agent provide bounded execution or evidence within their existing authority.
- **What:** activate Bridge summaries and Crew accountability for controlled alpha, preserve the internal-only Scrapbook prototype, keep unfinished L4/L5 concepts disabled, isolate the alpha Worker, produce teen and parent preview builds, and prove trust, deletion, isolation, and device journeys.
- **Where:** `jussray/Sekret-Bip`, draft PR #495, branch `launch/controlled-alpha-activation`, plus the dedicated `sekret-backend-alpha` runtime and controlled Supabase test accounts when explicitly approved.
- **When:** continue in Founder Room phase order. The hosted-runner startup failure remains classified evidence but is not allowed to freeze all repository-side preparation.
- **Why:** the app should expose what is actually built without pretending unfinished concepts are complete or weakening teen and parent boundaries.
- **How:** Observe → Orient → Artifact Plan → Execute → Verify → Founder Decide. Add missing artifacts and requirements to the ordered ledger as they are discovered. Never silently skip a phase or convert approval into execution proof.

## Activation boundary

### Activate for controlled alpha

- teen and parent onboarding and authentication;
- existing companion reply and voice paths through the canonical Worker contract;
- Teen Room, Pages, Calm, Circle, More, rewards, and reset surfaces;
- parent routes and relationship lifecycle;
- Bridge creation, summary-only parent visibility, revoke, re-share, unlink, and cleanup;
- Crew accountability, encouragement, revocation, and isolation;
- internal Emotional Scrapbook review;
- isolated alpha Worker and teen/parent preview builds.

### Keep blocked

- durable L4 companion memory;
- L5 cross-companion synthesis;
- any capability without schema, authorization, denial, deletion, rollback, and real journey proof;
- unrestricted public rollout;
- any parent access to raw teen content.

## Ordered execution

1. **Observe:** lock scope, exact state, non-claims, and preserved constraints.
2. **Orient:** map dependencies, red-team privacy and false-green risks, and identify the smallest reversible next slice.
3. **Artifact Plan:** assign one owner, evidence path, rollback, and founder gate to every required artifact.
4. **Execute:** prepare and, only after approval, provision the dedicated alpha runtime and preview builds without changing production rollout.
5. **Verify:** run Bridge, Crew, deletion, isolation, accessibility, failure-state, and device journeys with retained evidence.
6. **Founder Decide:** present ship, hold, or rollback options with exact remaining prohibitions.

## Non-claims

This brief is plan evidence. It is not a merge, deployment, credential operation, database mutation, preview build, device proof, two-account proof, deletion proof, or launch approval.

Nothing is deleted merely to simplify scope.
